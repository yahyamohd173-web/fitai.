PRIVACY

Privacy at FITAI.

This MVP stores preferences and saved looks in your browser. Uploaded photos are processed only for the styling request when the AI feature is enabled.

What we store

Demo preferences, saved outfits and demo login details may be stored in localStorage on your device. Do not upload sensitive or private images.

Photos

The demo compresses photos in the browser before sending a styling request. A production version should use private storage, explicit deletion controls and a clear retention policy.

Shopping links

Retailer links may open third-party websites. Their privacy policies and terms apply there.

Your control

You can remove saved outfits and reset preferences from the site. For production launch, add a formal deletion request workflow.
